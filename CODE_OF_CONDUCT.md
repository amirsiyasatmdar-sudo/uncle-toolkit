# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The Uncle Toolkit community aims to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and focused on learning and responsible software development.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical disagreements constructively.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith criticism.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's personal information.
- Keep participation inclusive and focused on the project.
- Follow the project's Acceptable Use Policy and applicable laws.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, abuse, or other unlawful activity.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub. Do not include sensitive personal information in public Issues.

The maintainers will review reports fairly and may edit or remove content, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation, or remove a participant from project spaces when necessary. Decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and the safety of the community.

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing the project.
