# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

The Uncle Toolkit community aims to be welcoming, respectful, constructive, and focused on learning and responsible software development.

We are committed to maintaining a community where participants can contribute, share ideas, and discuss technical matters without harassment or discrimination.

## Expected Behavior

All participants are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and technical disagreements constructively.
- Give actionable feedback and accept good-faith criticism.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's personal information without consent.
- Keep participation inclusive and focused on the project.
- Follow the project's Acceptable Use Policy and applicable laws.
- Act responsibly when interacting with other community members.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory conduct.
- Personal attacks, insults, or deliberate disruption.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Encouraging unauthorized access, abuse, or other unlawful activity.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through GitHub.

Do not include sensitive personal information or exploitable details in public Issues.

Maintainers will review reports fairly and handle them as confidentially as reasonably possible.

Depending on the circumstances and severity of the behavior, enforcement actions may include:

- Editing or removing inappropriate content.
- Issuing a warning.
- Temporarily restricting participation.
- Removing a participant from project spaces when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should be proportionate to the behavior and the safety of the community. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to project spaces and public interactions when someone is representing the Uncle Toolkit project.

All participants are expected to follow these standards when contributing to or interacting with the project community.
